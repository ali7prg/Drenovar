Reading:
- H1 (304 stalls): not on Cloudflare: its 304s carry no Content-Length and no Transfer-Encoding;
  all 91 of them in these runs completed (median 60–103 ms, longest 310 ms), none waited for a body. 1023 also hardens a 304/204 with a stray framing header
  (qa_api_pool: 1022 waited 8 s on such an answer, 1023 14 ms).
- H2 (dead kept-alive links): confirmed. 1022 waited the full 12 s timeout on a frozen link and
  showed the pill; 1023 abandons a reused link that has not answered in 3.5 s, retries once on a
  fresh connection (the request succeeds) and drops links idle for over 20 s or after a resume.
- H3 (stalls counted as an outage): the meter now discounts frames over 250 ms and time in the
  background (qa_net_1023: a 9.5 s frame or 11 s in the background after one failure shows no
  pill; 10 s of real silence still does).
- H4 (forest only while idle): confirmed (93.5 s, after the finger lifted); fixed (3.7–4.1 s with
  the finger down). The two warm-up frames of after_touch (330 and 214 ms) did not repeat in
  after_touch2 (nothing over 55 ms after the first state).
