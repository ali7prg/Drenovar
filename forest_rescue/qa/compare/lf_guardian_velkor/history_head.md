## Original vs new direction

Original `VELKOR_L1..L3_RIGGED_ANIMATED.glb`: identical grey-green procedural prototype (39.2k tris in 25
rigid section meshes, 13 joints, 5.0 m long, 5.68 m tall because of two tall, thin, vertical antlers, green
glowing rune discs on the flanks, moss blobs), facing +X.  New direction (art/11 top row): massive shaggy
elk-bison with long hanging brown hair, moss and leaves on the back/neck, a wide low 12+ tine rack with
hanging moss vines, beard, cloven hooves.  Kept: species id, 3 levels (game scale 1.02+0.16*level),
clip names, ~5 m footprint (new 4.97 m nose to tail).  The glowing runes of the prototype are not part of
the new direction and were dropped.
