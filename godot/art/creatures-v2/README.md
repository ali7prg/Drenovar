# DRENOVAR creatures-v2 — reproducible creature pipeline

Four game creatures rebuilt from the 17.11.2026 reference kit (`DRENOVAR_Creatures_Claude_Code.zip`):
**Лесной Велькор `velkor`, Тенерис `tenerys`, Камнеклык `kamneklyk`, Горный Драгор `dragor`**.
The game ids, encounters, levels, saves, server commands and rewards are unchanged; the new models are
drawn for the same encounters (`src/creature_visuals.gd`, `assets/creatures-v2/registry.json`).

Everything here is generated by code: no third-party model, texture or animation is used.
Tools used at build time only: Blender 5.3 as a Python module (`bpy`: QuadriFlow retopology,
decimation, MikkTSpace tangents, Cycles normal/AO bakes), `xatlas` (UV charts), `scikit-image`
(marching cubes), NumPy, Pillow. Their licences (GPL/MIT/BSD) do not apply to the produced assets.

## Pipeline

| Stage | Module | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Sculpt | `sdf.py`, `common.py`, `species/<id>.py` | Signed-distance sculpt from named primitives (capsules, tapered cones, ellipsoids, boxes, carves) tagged with their bone and surface class; fur locks, bark/slate/rock plates, antlers, tusks, fangs, claws, eyes as separate parts. Dimensions follow `02_CREATURES.txt` (metres, +Y up, +X forward). |
| High-res mesh | `geometry.py` | Narrow-band evaluation + marching cubes per part (1–1.8 cm body, 2.5–9 mm details). |
| Retopology / LOD | `geometry.py`, `blender_ops.py` | LOD0 body: QuadriFlow quads snapped back onto the sculpt; LOD1/LOD2: quadric decimation (silhouette kept). Rigid parts decimated, sharp edges kept. |
| UV | `blender_ops.uv_xatlas` | xatlas charts per material atlas, packed per LOD. |
| Bake | `blender_ops.bake_normal/bake_ao` | Cycles tangent-space normal (high-res sculpt -> low poly, per part) and AO. |
| Texture | `texturing.py`, `shading.py`, `raster.py` | Own UV rasteriser gives every texel its rest position, normal and MikkTSpace frame; species shading functions produce BaseColor, roughness/metallic, emissive, a detail height (fur strands, bark grain, slate, scales) blended onto the baked normal (RNM), AO x cavity, and the level-variant mask. |
| Fur cards | `furcards.py`, `species/<id>.py` (`FUR_CARDS`, `FUR_KEEP`) | Alpha-masked hair locks rooted on the outer sculpt (locks included), laid along the coat flow per region (mane, ruff, back ridge, legs, tail...); silhouette tufts get a second crossed strip. Both faces are explicit geometry with the outward surface normal (single-sided material, no back-face darkening in Godot/three.js/DCC). Vertex colour goes from the body BaseColor at the root to the region's tip colour; a strand atlas (pointed, split, wispy locks; a glow column whose strands are emissive). Card share, width and segments per LOD keep the triangle budgets; weights copied from the nearest body vertex. |
| Rig / skin | `rig.py`, `export.py` | Skeleton per species (CreatureRoot -> MotionRoot -> Pelvis -> Spine_01/02 -> Chest -> Neck_01/02 -> Head -> Jaw; Shoulder/Hip -> UpperLeg -> LowerLeg -> Foot -> Toe; Tail_01..; ears, mane, belly, digit helpers) and sockets. Skin weights from the sculpt primitives' ownership (softmin), smoothed on the surface, 4 influences; rigid parts on one bone. |
| Animation | `anim.py`, `clips.py`, `species/<id>.py` | Keyed poses (Hermite tracks), analytic leg IK with planted hooves/paws, gait generator (in place, stance feet move back at `speed_mps`), controller-consistent foot planning for turns/charge/pounce/dodge, spring tails/ears, death with per-frame ground contact on the skinned mesh. |
| Export | `gltf.py`, `export.py` | Core glTF 2.0 + `KHR_materials_emissive_strength` only (no Draco/Meshopt/KTX2). |
| Publish | `publish.py`, `write_import.py` | Copies the Godot runtime asset into `godot/project/assets/creatures-v2/` and writes `registry.json` (display adapter, body outline, LOD distance, level parameters, masks). |

## Outputs per species

* `<id>_lod0.glb` (close-up, 2K BaseColor/Normal + 1K ORM/Emissive), `<id>_lod1.glb` (field/mobile,
  1K + 512), `<id>_lod2.glb` (far, 512 + 256) — each self-contained with skeleton and all clips.
* `<ID>_L1/L2/L3_RIGGED_ANIMATED.glb` — LOD1 with the level look baked in (same file names the v92
  web client requests from `/creature-assets37/`).
* Godot runtime: `<id>_runtime.gltf/.bin/<id>_textures/` — LOD1 + LOD2 on one skeleton, level II/III
  overlay meshes (`*_VARIANT_L2/L3`), one copy of the clips.
* `clip_manifest.json` — names, frames (30 fps), loop, events (footsteps, windup/contact/recovery,
  sounds), attack windows, controller `motion` curves, `speed_mps`, semantic map and server events.

| | velkor | kamneklyk | tenerys | dragor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clips | 12 | 12 | 14 | 14 |
| Bones (skin joints) | 41 | 40 | 44 | 48 |
| LOD0 / LOD1 / LOD2 triangles | 93 748 / 37 288 / 8 740 | 76 440 / 30 536 / 8 072 | 58 736 / 24 476 / 6 230 | 72 578 / 27 378 / 7 430 |
| Materials per LOD | 3 (Body, AntlerBark, FurCards) | 3 (Body, Stone, FurCards) | 3 (Body, Details, FurCards) | 2 (Skin, Stone) |

Shapes, materials and attack keys follow the creature-references sheets (section below). The fur cards, moss
strands and denser plates are what goes over the 03 budgets (Velkor LOD1 37 288 against 20 000, Kamneklyk
30 536 / 22 000, Tenerys 24 476 / 19 000; Dragor is within budget); `FUR_KEEP` per LOD is the knob to trade
coat density for triangles.

* `deliver/<id>/` — per creature: `<id>.blend` (Blender 5.3, LOD1 meshes, armature, every clip as an action,
  PBR materials with packed textures; `tools/dcc_blend.py`), `<id>_animated.glb` (LOD1, all clips, level I look)
  and `clip_manifest.json`. LOD0 / LOD2 and the L1–L3 web files are rebuilt by the pipeline (not stored).

## Rebuild

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install bpy==5.3.0 numpy pillow scikit-image xatlas
PY=.venv/bin/python WORK=/tmp/cv2 godot/art/creatures-v2/tools/full_build.sh velkor   # ~10 min
.venv/bin/python -I godot/art/creatures-v2/publish.py /tmp/cv2 godot/project velkor
python3 godot/art/creatures-v2/write_import.py godot/project velkor
(cd godot/project && godot --headless --path . --import && python3 ../tools/texture_import_policy.py . && godot --headless --path . --import)
(cd godot/project && godot --headless --path . -s qa_creatures_v2.gd)
```

Kit-sheet match (previous references, kept for history): `tools/refmatch.py` segments the front / left side / rear views of the kit sheets
(`refs`), rasterises the sculpt in the same views (`model`, coarse meshes, IoU after a best fit of scale
and offset) and compares orthographic renders of the built model with the sheets (`compare`, from
`tools/media.gd --what=views`): `<id>_compare.jpg` puts sheet, model and the silhouette overlay side by
side (red: only on the sheet, blue: only on the model). Landmarks are read in metres off the sheet at
112 / 127 / 145 / 80 px per metre (Velkor / Tenerys / Kamneklyk / Dragor).

## creature-references (drenovar-server, branch `creature-references`)

The current shapes, materials and attack keys follow `creature-references/manifest.json` and its images: one
turnaround `model.png` per creature (side / front / rear, Dragar and Teneris also top) and four attack poses in
the manifest's order (`01-preparation`, `02-launch`, `03-contact`, `04-recovery`). The art ids `dragar` and
`teneris` map to the project ids `dragor` and `tenerys`.

```bash
R=<checkout of creature-references>/creature-references        # manifest.json + 20 PNG
PY=.venv/bin/python
$PY -I tools/refcheck.py refs $R /tmp/rc                        # masks, ground lines, px/m of every view
$PY -I tools/proof.py sculpt <id> /tmp/rc /tmp/proof            # coarse sculpt + fur strips in the sheet views
$PY -I tools/proof.py glb <id> <work>/<id>/out/<id>_lod0.glb /tmp/rc /tmp/glb idle:0   # built model, Cycles
$PY -I tools/proof.py glbposes <id> <work>/<id>/out/<id>_lod0.glb /tmp/rc /tmp/glb     # the 4 attack poses
$PY -I tools/proof.py cycle <id> /tmp/rc /tmp/cycles idle,walk,run                     # filmstrips
$PY -I tools/slide_check.py <id> [clip,...]                     # foot sliding before export
python3 -I tools/detail.py /tmp/rc /tmp/glb model_idle_0 <id> /tmp/glb side,front,rear,top,pose   # close-ups
python3 -I tools/refsheet.py /tmp/rc /tmp/glb <audit images> <audit measurements> <id> ...
```

* **Scale.** Every sheet is read at one metric scale from the manifest: Velkor 246.7 px/m (nose to tail tip
  3.6 m), Teneris 225.6 (3.95 m), Kamneklyk 315 (snout to rump 3.0 m), Dragar 121.6 (no numbers in the
  manifest: the dorsal scutes over the shoulders at 3.1 m, the shoulder height of the kit sheet with its 1.8 m
  man). Front / rear / top views are drawn with their own perspective; each is read at the scale where it agrees
  with the side view as orthographic views must (same highest point, same nose-to-tail length).
* **Renders.** Blender 5.3 Cycles, orthographic, at the sheet's own px/m, the model's ground on the sheet's
  ground line; only a horizontal shift is searched. Overlay: red - only the reference, cyan - only the model.
  Attack poses: one perspective camera per creature (azimuth / elevation / distance fitted on the recovery
  pose), the same camera for all four poses; the controller travel of charge / pounce is left out because the
  pose images are framed on the creature.
* **Proportions.** `warp.py` re-proportions a built creature with one smooth space warp (skeleton, sculpt
  primitives, sockets and fur-card regions together; rigid parts move without shear; ops can be limited to
  bones, e.g. the Teneris tail warp leaves the hind feet that stand under it). Species set `WARP`; the
  shading functions receive unwarped texel positions. Parts marked `"warp": False` are authored directly in
  the new proportions (the Velkor antler crown).
* **Close-ups.** `tools/detail.py` cuts the same regions from the reference and from the render aligned to it
  (`proof.py` writes `<id>_<tag>_<view>_aligned.png` and `<id>_glbpose_<phase>_aligned.png`): head, back, legs and
  tail from the side, head / chest / feet from the front, rear, top halves, and head / body on the attack poses
  (the 16 pose images double as 3/4 close-ups). `refsheet.py` collects them into `closeup_<id>.jpg`.
* **Eyes.** An eye sphere has to break the head surface and its socket cut has to open through it: Teneris and
  Kamneklyk had their eyes closed in under 1-2.5 cm of skull until the close-up pass (check the body SDF along the
  eye axis, as in that pass).
* **Leg fur.** Card seeds are random points in a region projected onto the nearest surface, so a region centred on
  the midline roots the leg cards on the inner faces of the legs only. Leg regions are per leg, centred outside it.
* **Plates.** `sdf.Prim(grp=...)`: the cut-outs of a plate / shard / scute carve only that plate, never the
  neighbours it overlaps. Baked normals on rigid parts tilted more than ~70 degrees from the low poly are bake
  errors (a ray hit a neighbouring plate) and are replaced by the surface normal (`texturing.run`).
* **Stance changes are steps.** `clips.step_offsets` moves a foot to a new stance only while it is lifted (an
  extra quick step, or riding on a planned swing), so a wide attack stance never slides the feet.
* **Footprint.** `FOOTPRINT_X_MIN` (Dragor -3.5 m) leaves the far part of a long, low tail out of the battle
  footprint, so `display_scale` keeps the body's in-game size and the tail trails behind the old outline.

Checks: `tools/validate.mjs` (Khronos glTF-Validator), `tools/run_three.cjs` + `tools/index.html`
(Three.js r171 GLTFLoader in Chromium), `tools/foot_check.py` (foot sliding / ground contact from
the exported GLB), `tools/capture_battle.gd` (real game scene from a server simulator battle),
`tools/media.gd` (portraits, turntables, clip frames from the game project).
